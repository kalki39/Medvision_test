about me

I'm Dr. Banurekha, CPC, CPC-I (Instructor), CCS – the Founder and CEO of MedVision Health Solutions. With over 20 years of healthcare experience and 15+ years of focused expertise in medical coding, I established MedVision with a singular goal: to train candidates into perfect medical coders, not just coders.

MedVision is proud to be a pioneer in medical coding training, offering custom-built, state-of-the-art programs tailored to the needs of graduates, doctors, professors, and professionals. Over the years, we've successfully trained more than 10,000 students in specialties such as ED, E&M, Surgery, Anesthesia, Radiology, and more.

At MedVision, we don’t just teach—we mentor with passion to ensure every candidate builds a thriving career. With consistent 100% placement assistance, we are associated with top institutions across Coimbatore and beyond.

Let’s build your future, perfectly coded.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver high-quality, customized medical coding education that equips students, graduates, and healthcare professionals with the skills and confidence to succeed in the healthcare industry. We focus on specialty-based training, real-time learning, and 100% placement support to help our learners build fulfilling careers.

Our Vision

We envision a future where medical coding training is synonymous with quality, precision, and professional growth. By combining expert-led instruction with innovative learning methods, we strive to be the most trusted name in medical coding education, transforming aspiring learners into perfect coders.

Passion, precision, and purpose guide everything we do. At MedVision, we value:

  • Excellence in education

  • We believe in nurturing coders who are not just job-ready, but career-ready.
