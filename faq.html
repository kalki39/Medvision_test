our FAQ

What is the eligibility to join the Medical Coding course at Medvision?

Anyone who has completed a degree in Life Sciences, Paramedical, Allied Health Sciences, Pharmacy, Nursing, or any related field can join. Even non-life science graduates can enroll after a basic medical terminology training.

Does Medvision provide CPC certification training?

Yes, we offer specialized CPC (Certified Professional Coder) certification training, exam preparation support, and mock tests to help students clear their certification confidently.

Will I get placement support after completing the course?

Absolutely! Medvision provides placement assistance through partnerships with reputed healthcare companies and medical coding firms, ensuring maximum job opportunities for our students.

Are both online and offline classes available?

Yes, Medvision offers flexible learning options — students can choose between classroom training at our Coimbatore center or online live sessions based on their convenience.

How long is the Medical Coding course, and what is the fee structure?

The course duration typically ranges from 2 to 3 months, depending on the batch type (regular/fast track). Fee details can be obtained by contacting our admissions team — we also offer easy installment options.
