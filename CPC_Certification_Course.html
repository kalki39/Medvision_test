What is CPC Certification?

The CPC (Certified Professional Coder) certification by AAPC is the gold standard for medical coding professionals. It validates your expertise in coding medical procedures, diagnoses, and services using CPT®, ICD-10-CM, and HCPCS Level II code sets. A CPC-certified coder is highly valued in hospitals, physician offices, insurance companies, and healthcare consultancies worldwide.

Who is a Certified Professional Coder (CPC)?

A Certified Professional Coder is a trained expert who assigns standardized codes for diagnoses and medical services to ensure accurate billing and healthcare documentation. CPC-certified coders are recognized for their high-level knowledge, attention to detail, and compliance with medical coding guidelines.
