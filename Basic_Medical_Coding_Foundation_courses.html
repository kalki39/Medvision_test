What is Medical Coding?

Medical coding is the process of converting healthcare diagnoses, procedures, medical services, and equipment into standardized alphanumeric codes. These codes are used for billing, insurance claims, record-keeping, and maintaining patient histories. Medical coding forms the backbone of healthcare management, ensuring hospitals, clinics, and doctors are accurately reimbursed and patient care data is properly documented.

Who is a Medical Coder?

A Medical Coder is a trained professional who reads patient charts and clinical documents, analyzes them, and assigns the appropriate codes following healthcare coding guidelines like ICD-10-CM, CPT, and HCPCS. Medical coders play a critical role in healthcare systems, helping streamline processes, maintain compliance, and ensure proper revenue flow.
